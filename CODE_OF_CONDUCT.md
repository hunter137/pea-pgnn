# Code of conduct

PEA-PGNN is committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project
community.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- welcome questions and contributors with different levels of experience;
- give actionable technical feedback and acknowledge uncertainty;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses, research ethics, and data-use limits;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when reviewing contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation of another person's work, and sustained disruptive conduct
are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For routine disagreements, first try to resolve the matter calmly in the
relevant issue or pull request. For harassment, threats, privacy concerns, or a
report that should not be public, contact the maintainer through the
[`hunter137` GitHub profile](https://github.com/hunter137) and request a private
channel. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as
practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is
not permitted.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where a person is representing the project.
